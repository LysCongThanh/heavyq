**`heavyq flow`: a usage limit is one fleet entry that clears, and gone lanes drop out.** When agents hit the usage limit (a StopFailure `rate_limit`, or a `quota_auto_resume_*` notification) the state now has one `quota` entry and one `needs_you` line, "quota hit at 19:20, 4 agents stopped, resets ~23:20" (the reset time from the status line's `rate_limits`, which heavyq now records with `resets_at`), instead of a `blocked_human` lane per agent. It clears on the next activity of any session in any repository, or when the status line shows the window reset. A per-lane block now also clears when the same session shows life under another lane (the lane follows the work tree an agent may leave), the `SessionStart`, `UserPromptSubmit` and `Stop` hooks count as activity, and a lane whose work tree no longer exists drops out of `lanes` and `needs_you`. See `docs/flow.md`.
