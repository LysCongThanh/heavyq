**`heavyq flow`: where work waits, derived from facts.** Per-item timelines (queued, working, proving, rework, blocked on a person, done proven or claimed unproven) come from the ledger, the leases and the hooks; `heavyq flow show [--json]` prints the materialised state (needs-you, the constraint, proven items per active hour) from `<git common dir>/heavyq/flow/state.json`, written atomically at most once a minute at the end of any run and at the SessionStart, Stop and notification hooks, and never at PreToolUse. The new `flow` hook feature (`heavyq hook install claude --features flow`, also in the plugin) observes permission requests, idle prompts, stop failures and model switches, and never decides anything. Ledger records gain optional `agent` and `model`, joined from a per-session record kept by `heavyq hook claude-sessionmeta` (status-line JSON on stdin). See [docs/flow.md](docs/flow.md).
