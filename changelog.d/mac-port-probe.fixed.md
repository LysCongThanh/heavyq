**A port pool no longer hands out a port a wildcard listener holds on macOS.** The probe only tried to listen on the loopback address, and on macOS and the BSDs that succeeds beside a `0.0.0.0` or `[::]` listener (Go sets `SO_REUSEADDR`); it now also tries to connect, which finds any listener on every platform. The test suite also passes on a Mac reached through `--remote`, where the temporary directory is a symlink (`/var` to `/private/var`).
