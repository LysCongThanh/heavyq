**`item claim` refuses what an agent cannot finish and prove.** `heavyq item claim <id>` and `claim --next` now refuse an item with no runnable proof, or whose proof's ledger p50 duration times the expected iterations is above the 30 minute lease (three 11 minute proofs do not fit one lease). The refusal exits 1 with a short reason and a `next:` line, `split <id> into ≤3 items, each with a faster proof`, with the command that opens the anchor at that line; `--json` prints a `refused` object. `--force` claims anyway, `--next` passes oversized items over and only refuses when nothing else is left, and a proof that never passed here has an unknown duration and is allowed with a warning. The limits are `[items] iterations` (default 3) and `max_proof_share` (default 1) in `heavyq.toml`. See [docs/items.md](docs/items.md).
